# Wonderweave — First Mystery Solution Guide

**Status:** Proposed  
**Governs:** Hints, intended resolution, and review criteria for *The Unfinished Map*; not approved or playtested.  
**Last verified:** 2026-10-10

**Spoiler warning:** Keep this guide separate from the player-facing draft.

### Hint 1

Use the station notes to place the cards. Then compare what each record says happened at the center.

### Hint 2

Track the chalk, seed packets, and cord across the records.

### Hint 3

Useful things are left at the center, used by different people, and returned. What might that suggest about how the place is used?

### Intended resolution

**Station arrangement:** The notes give Lantern → Seed, Seed → Bell, and Bell → River. Since the four positions form a ring, River leads back to Lantern. The cycle is Lantern → Seed → Bell → River → Lantern, unique up to rotation.

**Evidence at the center:** Chalk is used and returned, seed packets are left and one is taken for a practical purpose, and cord is left, used, and returned. The center has a shelf, and the mapmaker leaves it without a label or boundary. Together, these details support the interpretation that the center functions as a shared-use place where useful things can be left and taken.

**Limits of the evidence:** The records do not prove who owns the land, whether absolutely everyone is welcome, or what the mapmaker privately intended. The solution should explain the shared-use interpretation as a supported inference, not an established fact.

## Review notes — not player-facing

- This is an assembled text draft, not a designed visual artifact or a tested game.
- Check whether the station arrangement feels like a small discovery rather than a worksheet.
- Check whether the shared-use interpretation is discovered from the records rather than guessed from the question.
- Ask cold readers to cite the details that support their interpretation and to name what remains uncertain.
- If readers only repeat a general moral about sharing, the second layer has not worked.
- Create the final map and station-card artwork only after the text and logic survive cold-reader review.
