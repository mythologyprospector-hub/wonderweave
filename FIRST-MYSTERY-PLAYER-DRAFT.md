## The Unfinished Map

The mapmaker left a map with four stations around an empty center. Four records describe the stations and things that happened at the center.

You can read the records in any order.

Arrange the four stations around the ring using the station notes. Then read the records in that order. **What do the records, taken together, suggest about the empty center?**

## The map

The map has four equally spaced sockets around a ring. The center is blank.

```text
                  [       ]
                 /         \\
        [       ]     ○     [       ]
                 \\         /
                  [       ]
```

The brackets are station sockets; the circle is the empty center. The lines show the paths connecting neighboring stations around the ring, without crossing the center.

**Clockwise** means moving around the ring in the same direction as the hands of a clock: from 12 to 3, then 6, then 9, and back to 12.

## The four records

### Record A — Lantern Station

**Station note:** Seed comes immediately after Lantern when moving clockwise.

A piece of chalk is kept on the stone shelf at the center. I used it to mark a loose stone on the path, then returned it to the shelf.

### Record B — Seed Station

**Station note:** Bell comes immediately after Seed when moving clockwise.

I borrowed the chalk from the center shelf to mark three planting rows and returned it. I left a small bundle of seed packets on the shelf.

### Record C — Bell Station

**Station note:** River comes immediately after Bell when moving clockwise.

I took one seed packet from the bundle to repair a bare patch beside the river path. Before leaving, I placed a coil of spare cord on the center shelf.

### Record D — River Station

I used the cord from the center shelf to mark a safe crossing, then returned it. The mapmaker drew the four stations and their connecting paths but left the center without a name or boundary.

## Before you finish

- What clockwise order is supported by the station notes?
- When you read the records in that order, what patterns do you notice in the objects?
- What does the evidence support—and what does it leave uncertain?

There is no penalty for taking your time or changing your mind. You may solve this alone or discuss it with others.
